Middlegame: finish what you start

The winning checklist

Run through this every move once you're ahead. It takes ten seconds, and you have the time: you usually finish with over 6 minutes left.

  1. What does my opponent threaten? Checks, captures, attacks on my pieces. Deal with it first.
  2. Is anything of mine undefended? Loose pieces are where tactics start.
  3. Can I take something for free? Then check twice that it really is free.
  4. Trade pieces, not pawns. Every trade makes your extra material count for more.
  5. No hurry. If nothing is urgent, improve your worst piece or make your king safer. Simple beats brilliant.
