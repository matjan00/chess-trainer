# COURSE jobava | Jobava London | white
sub: 1.d4, 2.Nc3 and 3.Bf4: one simple attacking set-up for White against almost everything
why: The Jobava London gives you the same first three moves in nearly every game, so you spend your time on plans, not on memorising. The knight on c3 and the bishop on f4 work together against c7, and many Black players at your level have never seen it. Against best play the position is about equal, but you know the plans and they usually don't. You already reach these positions often: in your games 1.d4 d5 is by far Black's most common answer.

## CHAPTER c5 | Black hits the centre: 3...c5
> Black attacks your d4 pawn at once. The rule: play e3 first, and if Black takes on d4, take back with the e-pawn. Then develop quickly and use the e5 square.

### LINE c5-main | Main line: 4...cxd4 5.exd4 a6 | main
