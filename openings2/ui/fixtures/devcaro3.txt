## RULES
rule: h-pawn | h4 against your bishop | On f5 answer ...h5 so the pawn stops; on g6 answer ...h6, because the bishop needs h7 to escape.
rule: f7-watch | A bishop looks at f7 | When White's bishop sits on c4, shut the diagonal with ...e6.
rule: trade-it | Trade a bishop that is hit | When your bishop is attacked on f5 by a bishop, take it and stay solid.
rule: develop-first | Develop to guard d5 | When White hits d5, bring a piece out that guards it before anything else.
