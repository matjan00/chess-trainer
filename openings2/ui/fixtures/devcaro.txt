> White grabs space. Get the bishop out, then hit the centre with ...c5.

## CHAPTER cl | The Classical: 3.Nc3
> White develops. Take on e4 and bring the bishop to f5.

### LINE cl-main | Classical: 4...Bf5 5.Ng3 Bg6 | main
